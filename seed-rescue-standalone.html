Seed Rescue

Recover your own wallet when a recovery phrase has a missing, misplaced or misspelled word. Everything runs on this device and nothing is sent anywhere.

Example loaded: a public test phrase with its last word missing.

Type ? for a missing word and ~word for a word you have but in the wrong place. Up to two ? is practical.

Required. A phrase counts as found only when it produces an address you already know belongs to you.

Address types

The type is taken from the address you enter. Fix typos tries close matches for words that are not on the BIP39 list.

Every word is correct but the order is unknown. 8 positions take seconds, 10 take minutes, 12 take hours.

If you are sure of some positions, leave them out. Fewer positions means a much faster search.
